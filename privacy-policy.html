Last updated: August 20, 2026

Children's Privacy

Cici Run is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us unintentionally, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Third-Party Services

The released version of Cici Run may use third-party services necessary for app functionality, analytics, advertising, or distribution. Any such services will be reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game or its services change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.

Contact

For privacy-related questions about Cici Run, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on its Google Play listing.
